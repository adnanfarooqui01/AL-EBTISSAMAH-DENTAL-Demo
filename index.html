DENTAL TIPS

Small habits, healthier smile.

Simple advice from our clinic to help you look after your teeth at home.

Why flossing matters

Brushing alone misses the spaces between teeth. Flossing removes plaque and food particles, helping prevent gum disease and cavities.

Foods that strengthen teeth

Dairy, leafy greens, nuts and crunchy fruit support strong teeth and gums. Limit sugary snacks and drinks.

Overcoming dental anxiety

You are not alone. Tell us how you feel, ask us to explain each step, and take breaks whenever you need.
